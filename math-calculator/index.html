Core Mathematical Features & Formulas

1. Fractions & Decimals (S ⇄ D)

Our calculator supports automatic rational fraction reduction. Tap the S ⇄ D toggle button to convert decimal outputs like 0.375 to irreducible fractions like 3/8.

0.875 ⇄ 7/8 | 1.66667 ⇄ 5/3

2. Trigonometric Angles (DEG vs RAD)

Toggle between Degrees (default for school geometry and physics) and Radians (standard for calculus and engineering).

3. Quadratic Equation Solver

Solves equations in the standard form \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) using the quadratic formula:

4. Descriptive Statistics

Computes central tendency (Mean, Median, Mode) and dispersion metrics (Sample Variance \(s^2\) and Standard Deviation \(\sigma\)).

s = √(Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1))

5. Modulo Operator (mod)

The Modulo operation finds the remainder after dividing two integers. In mathematics, A mod B answers: "When A is divided by B, what is left over?"

10 mod 3 = 1 25 mod 4 = 1 17 mod 5 = 2

6. Modulus / Absolute Value (|x|)

The Modulus (Absolute Value) is the non-negative distance of a number from zero on the number line. Any negative input becomes positive.

|-3| = 3 |5 - 9| = 4 |-15.5| = 15.5

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate Modulus / Absolute Value (e.g. |-3| = 3)?

You can simply type |-3| or tap the |x| button in the scientific tray. It automatically wraps your number or expression in vertical modulus bars and returns its positive value. For example, |-3| = 3, |5 - 9| = 4, and |-15.5| = 15.5.

What does "mod" mean and how do I use it?

mod stands for Modulo. It calculates the remainder (शेषफल) after dividing two numbers. To use it, enter two numbers separated by mod, for example: 10 mod 3 (which equals 1). It is essential in coding, clock arithmetic, and modular math.

Can I use my physical computer keyboard to type calculations?

Yes! The input field supports direct typing of numbers, standard arithmetic operators (+, -, *, /), parentheses, and function names like sin(45), sqrt(64), or log(100). Press Enter to calculate instantly.

Does this calculator work offline?

Yes. Once loaded in your browser, the computation engine runs 100% locally on your device without communicating with remote servers.

How do I calculate combinations (nCr) and permutations (nPr)?

Use the nCr or nPr buttons or type combinations(n, r) or permutations(n, r) in the expression box (for example: combinations(5, 2) = 10).
