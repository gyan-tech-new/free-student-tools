About StudentDocs

StudentDocs was created to solve a widespread, frustrating problem faced by millions of students, job aspirants, and educators every day: bloated, expensive, privacy-invasive online tools that limit file sizes, add watermarks, and demand paid subscriptions.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is simple: provide world-class, high-precision academic and document tools that are completely free, private, and accessible to everyone — regardless of internet bandwidth, device type, or financial background.

🔒 The Zero-Server Privacy Standard

When preparing passport photos for competitive government exams (like SSC, UPSC, NEET, Police, or Banking) or uploading college certificates, students are frequently forced to upload sensitive identification documents to unknown cloud servers.

At StudentDocs, we re-architected this paradigm: all image compression, PDF merging, text conversions, and mathematical computations execute 100% locally in your web browser using modern WebAssembly and Canvas pipelines. Your sensitive personal data never touches our servers.
