Official Indian Govt Exam Photo & Signature Specifications

Almost all major competitive exams in India have strict automated validation algorithms on their application portals. If your uploaded photograph or signature does not match their exact kilobyte (KB) range, the form rejects the file immediately.

Exam / Portal Name Photograph Criteria Signature Criteria Format
SSC (CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS) 20 KB to 50 KB (3.5 x 4.5 cm) 10 KB to 20 KB (4.0 x 2.0 cm) JPG / JPEG
UPSC (Civil Services / NDA / CDS) 20 KB to 50 KB 20 KB to 50 KB JPG / JPEG
Railway Recruitment (RRB NTPC, Group D) 20 KB to 50 KB 10 KB to 20 KB JPG / JPEG
Banking (IBPS PO, Clerk, SBI) 20 KB to 50 KB (200 x 230 px) 10 KB to 20 KB (140 x 60 px) JPG / JPEG
NEET / CUET / JEE (NTA) 10 KB to 100 KB 4 KB to 30 KB JPG / JPEG

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my photo get blurred or rejected by the portal?

No. The tool balances bicubic pixel sampling and JPEG quantization tables so your facial features, eyes, and signature strokes remain crisp and fully legible for biometric review.

What if my signature needs to be under 20 KB?

Simply click the 'Govt Signature (10-20 KB)' preset button above. The tool automatically sets the target size to 15 KB, which is accepted 100% of the time across all Indian portals.

Can I use a mobile phone selfie?

Yes! You can upload a photo taken with your smartphone (even if it is 5MB to 10MB in size). The tool will shrink it down to under 50KB in less than one second.
