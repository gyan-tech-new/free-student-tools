Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FreeDailyTools and how we operate

Are all tools on FreeDailyTools really 100% free? +

Yes. Every tool across all categories (Students, Creators, Finance, PDF, and Utilities) is completely free to use without registration, paid tiers, or artificial daily limits.

Do you store or look at my uploaded images or documents? +

Never. Our applications use modern HTML5 Canvas, Web Workers, and WebAssembly technologies that process files directly inside your browser. Because files are never sent over the internet to any server, it is physically impossible for us or third parties to access your data.

Can I use these tools on my mobile phone? +

Yes. All FreeDailyTools utilities are fully responsive and optimized for touchscreens on Android, iPhone, iPad, tablets, and desktop computers.

How do you support the website if everything is free? +

FreeDailyTools is supported by non-intrusive contextual online advertising. Because we do not run expensive cloud server processing pipelines for file operations, our hosting overhead is virtually zero, allowing us to keep all services completely free for users worldwide.
