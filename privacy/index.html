Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 • Last Updated: October 30, 2026

1. Our Core Commitment: 100% Client-Side Privacy

At StudentDocs (accessible from https://free-student-tools.vercel.app), the privacy of our student and educator visitors is our highest priority. Unlike conventional online file converters and calculators that upload user files to remote servers, all StudentDocs tools run 100% locally inside your web browser using modern client-side JavaScript, Canvas APIs, and WebAssembly.

🔒 Your passport photos, exam signatures, marksheets, academic PDFs, text notes, and mathematical expressions are NEVER transmitted to our servers or stored in any cloud database.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect names, phone numbers, or residential addresses.
  • We do not store or retain uploaded images, PDFs, or scanned documents.
  • All document conversions, compression routines, and PDF merges are performed in your browser's temporary memory and erased upon page refresh or closing.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

StudentDocs does not use tracking cookies to identify personal browsing habits. Some tools (such as the Math Calculator) may use browser localStorage purely to remember your recent calculation history or angle preference (DEG vs RAD) on your device. You can clear this history anytime via the "Clear History" button or your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We may partner with third-party service providers, such as content delivery networks (CDNs like Cloudflare, Tailwind CDN, and CDNJS) and advertising networks (such as Google AdSense). These third parties may use cookies or web beacons to serve advertisements based on prior visits to this or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or Google Ads Settings.

5. Children's Information

StudentDocs is designed to be safe, educational, and accessible to students of all ages. Because all document operations are strictly client-side and require zero account registration, we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Page or via email at support@studentdocs.org.
