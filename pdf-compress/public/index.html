Frequently Asked Questions

Will compressing my PDF make the text illegible?

No. Our Recommended mode preserves crisp letterforms, formulas, and diagrams while aggressively stripping invisible metadata and re-encoding heavy embedded images.

How much can I reduce my PDF file size?

Scanned PDFs containing mobile phone photos often shrink by 60% to 85% (e.g. from 12MB down to 1.5MB), making them instant email attachments.

Can I compress scanned handwriting notes?

Yes! Scanned handwriting notes benefit the most because modern smartphone cameras save photos at unnecessarily high resolutions (4K) which inflate PDF size.
