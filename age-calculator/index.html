How Govt Exam Age Eligibility & Cutoff Dates Work in India

Filling out application forms for competitive examinations such as SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, UPSC Civil Services, IBPS Bank PO/Clerk, Railway RRB, and State Police requires precise age calculation. Even a discrepancy of a single day can lead to form rejection at the document verification (DV) stage.

How Our Algorithm Computes Exact Age

Our calculator implements the statutory Gregorian chronological calendar algorithm:

  1. Day Borrowing: If the cutoff day is smaller than the birth day, days from the preceding month are borrowed based on the exact month length (28, 29, 30, or 31 days).
  2. Month Borrowing: If the cutoff month is smaller than the birth month, 12 months are borrowed from the cutoff year.
  3. Leap Year Correction: Computes whether February 29th was crossed in leap cycles to ensure zero calculation drift over decades.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which date is used as the proof of Date of Birth in government exams? +

In almost all Indian competitive examinations, the Date of Birth recorded in your Class 10th (Matriculation) or Secondary Education Board Certificate is accepted as conclusive proof. Birth certificates, Aadhaar cards, or affidavits are not accepted as substitute proof if they contradict the 10th marksheet.

What is the meaning of "Crucial Date for Age Calculation"? +

The "Crucial Date" is the fixed benchmark date specified in recruitment rules against which candidate age is determined. Even if you submit your application on 20th August, your age is determined as of the notification's designated crucial date (e.g. 1st August 2026).

Is age relaxation applicable to EWS (Economically Weaker Section) candidates? +

No. Under current Central Government recruitment policy, the 10% EWS reservation provides quota in vacancies and cutoffs, but does not grant age relaxation. EWS candidates must comply with the General/UR age limits.
